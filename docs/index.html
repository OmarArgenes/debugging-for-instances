Operational cycle

Seven phases from symptom to verified correction

DFI treats debugging as an iterative reduction of uncertainty, not as a sequence of guesses.

01

Delimit

Define the observable problem, environment and constraints.

02

Hypothesize

Create explanations that evidence can support or reject.

03

Select

Choose the next diagnostic test with useful information gain.

04

Observe

Instrument minimally and capture what the system actually does.

05

Update

Refine the hypothesis set instead of forcing an early conclusion.

06

Correct

Apply the smallest justified change to the supported cause.

07

Verify

Reproduce, regression-check and close with traceable evidence.
