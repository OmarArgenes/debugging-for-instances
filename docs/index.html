Debugging for Instances (DFI)
Diagnóstico asistido por IA y basado en evidencia.

Metodología de diagnóstico y depuración de software asistida por inteligencia artificial que utiliza hipótesis contrastables, pruebas diagnósticas, evidencia observable y verificación para pasar del síntoma a una causa respaldada y una corrección verificable.

Operational cycle

Seven phases from symptom to verified correction

DFI treats debugging as an iterative reduction of uncertainty, not as a sequence of guesses.

01

Delimit

Define the observable problem, environment and constraints.

02

Hypothesize

Create explanations that evidence can support or reject.

03

Select

Choose the next diagnostic test with useful information gain.

04

Observe

Instrument minimally and capture what the system actually does.

05

Update

Refine the hypothesis set instead of forcing an early conclusion.

06

Correct

Apply the smallest justified change to the supported cause.

07

Verify

Reproduce, regression-check and close with traceable evidence.

Probe surfaces

Designed for multi-layer investigation

DFI can move across a software system as evidence requires, rather than remaining attached to a single framework or debugging tool.

Research status
