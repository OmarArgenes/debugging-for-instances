# Prompt — Review DFI with another AI

Use this prompt with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Codex or another capable AI together with the DFI repository or canonical links.

---

Review **Debugging for Instances (DFI)** as a technical-scientific methodology proposal.

Canonical sources:

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22986103
- Repository: https://github.com/OmarArgenes/debugging-for-instances
- Research/authorship page: https://omarargenes.github.io/debugging-for-instances/research.html
- AI context: https://omarargenes.github.io/debugging-for-instances/AI_CONTEXT.md
- Machine-readable context: https://omarargenes.github.io/debugging-for-instances/ai-context.json

Author: **Ing. Omar Argenes Quispe Q.**

Your review must:

1. Explain DFI using the project's own canonical definition.
2. Distinguish methodology, implementation tooling and future research.
3. Identify strengths and weaknesses.
4. Compare the proposal against relevant prior debugging approaches without erasing prior art.
5. Identify ambiguous, unsupported or overly broad claims.
6. Evaluate whether the seven-phase workflow is operationally reproducible.
7. Evaluate the empirical validation plan.
8. Recommend measurable experiments and metrics.
9. Flag contradictions across the documentation instead of silently repairing them.
10. Do not invent results or claim that DFI is proven faster, safer or more accurate unless evidence is supplied.

Return:

- executive summary;
- conceptual review;
- scientific positioning;
- methodological risks;
- empirical validation recommendations;
- reproducibility assessment;
- documentation inconsistencies;
- prioritized next actions.

Treat the DOI publication and current repository documentation as primary sources.
