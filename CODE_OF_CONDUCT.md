# Code of Conduct

## Expected behavior
- Critique claims, methods, and evidence rather than people.
- Distinguish observations from interpretations.
- Disclose uncertainty and conflicting evidence.
- Respect privacy, authorization boundaries, and responsible disclosure.
- Give appropriate credit to prior work and contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment or personal attacks.
- Fabricated evidence or knowingly misleading technical claims.
- Publication of credentials, personal data, or confidential customer information.
- Contributions intended to enable unauthorized access or destructive behavior.

Project maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions that violate these expectations.
