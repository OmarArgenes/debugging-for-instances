# DFI Roadmap

## v1.0 — Foundation
- Formalize the methodology.
- Publish the foundational document with DOI.
- Establish canonical terminology, templates, and validation criteria.
- Create a public repository and static project site.

## v1.1 — Reproducible case corpus
- Publish sanitized, reproducible cases across multiple stacks.
- Add timing and diagnostic-path metrics.
- Improve case schema and evidence artifact conventions.

## Research phase
- Define a controlled incident corpus.
- Compare appropriate baseline debugging with DFI conditions.
- Report positive, negative, and inconclusive outcomes.
- Analyze threats to validity and replication quality.

## Future tooling
- Explore a DFI Analyzer concept for case capture, hypothesis tracking, evidence linking, and verification.
- Keep tooling separate from claims about the methodology itself.

Roadmap items are proposals and may change as empirical results become available.
